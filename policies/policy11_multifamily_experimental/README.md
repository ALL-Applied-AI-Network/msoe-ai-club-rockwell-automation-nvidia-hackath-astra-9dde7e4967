# Astra Policy 11

Policy 11 is a variable-count, prompt-routed composite policy for:

- M4 and M8 screws
- AA and AAA batteries
- round and long pills

The CSI instruction may route a complete family to one bin or route individual
subtypes separately. Supported destination colors are red, green, yellow, and
blue. Unmentioned families are not activated. Counts are discovered from the
image; 48 objects is a configurable safety ceiling, not an expected inventory.

See `POLICY11_RUNBOOK.md` before mock, submission, or physical execution.
